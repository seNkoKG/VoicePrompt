The shortest path from thought to text

Three moments. Zero ceremony.

No recording window, transcript editor, or copy-and-paste routine. VoicePrompt works around your current app.

Bilingual Auto

English and Slovenian, without a switch.

Language-neutral detection keeps normal English clean, understands standard or colloquial Slovenian, and verifies weak cross-language guesses before they reach your text.

ENSL Auto
Local by default

Your microphone is not a cloud service.

Speech recognition runs on your GPU. Audio is never stored. Optional text cleanup and self-hosted server modes are explicit choices.

Live feedback

A signal you can see.

The click-through overlay follows the active screen and responds to the actual microphone level.
